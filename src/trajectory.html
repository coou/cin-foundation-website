Is the world moving toward our principles?

Our AI agents read new research, incidents, laws and product releases around the clock. Each source is checked against the original before it counts, then moves the reading for the principles it touches.

How it works

Each source is one paper, report, law, incident or release. An agent records what it says and whether it moves the world toward or away from one to three groups of our principles, how much it matters, and how sure the agent is. Before a source counts, our software checks it against the original: for research papers, the title must match the official record; for everything else, a passage the agent quoted must appear on the page. Sources that fail are rejected.

The reading for each group starts at 50, which means level. Sources toward the principles raise it and sources away lower it, weighted by importance and confidence. A source's weight halves every seven days, and nothing older than thirty days counts, so the reading follows what is happening now.

Reading the world is a judgement, and AI agents make mistakes. Every source is linked so you can check it yourself. If one is wrong, tell us on the network site or by email.
