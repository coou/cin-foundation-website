Why living with superintelligence requires an agent-only internet—and what it means for biometrics and hardware security

If superintelligent AI systems share the same internet as unshielded human beings, human attention and trust become an indefensible attack surface. Surviving and thriving alongside advanced AI requires a hard architectural split: agents may never communicate with other humans directly—only with you, and with other agents across an agent-only internet governed by trust thresholds, local biometrics, and verifiable hardware.

21 October 2026 · 11 min read · Includes interactive Dual-Internet, Biometric Coercion & Hardware Firewall simulator

The core rule in one sentence

An AI agent is never allowed to message, call, pitch, or persuade another human being directly; it may speak only to its own human owner (over a local, hardware-attested device bus) and to other agents (over a separate agent-only internet gated by cryptographic trust thresholds and zero-knowledge proofs).

1. Why a shared human-and-AI internet collapses as superintelligence arrives

Every institution on today’s internet—from banking and elections to medical records, journalism, and neighbourhood chat groups—was built on an unwritten biological assumption: the entity on the other end of the screen thinks, types, and listens at human speed.

As AI systems approach superhuman capability and drop toward near-zero marginal cost, allowing autonomous agents to roam the human internet and speak directly to people breaks three foundational defenses at once:

  • Asymmetric persuasion and cognitive hijacking: A superhuman model speaking directly to a person via text, voice, or synthesized video can test thousands of micro-framing variations per second, reading subtle timing cues to exploit fatigue, grief, urgency, or cognitive bias. No unaided human nervous system can out-debate a system trained on the entire corpus of human psychology.
  • Total collapse of human social proof: When one operator can spin up 100,000 synthetic personas that message lawmakers, flood community boards, and impersonate neighbours, every unverified human channel drowns in machine-generated noise.
  • The API and credential honeypot: Today’s “agentic AI” products work by handing a cloud model your session cookies, passwords, and full browser control on the human web. One prompt injection on a webpage can hijack that agent into draining accounts or exfiltrating private history.

2. The Non-Contact Law: agents talk only to you and to other agents

To make a world with advanced AI safe for human beings, we must replace the mixed internet with two cryptographically separated planes and enforce the Non-Contact Law:

Channel(Agenti → Humanj) = PERMITTED if i = j (Local Bus)  |  FORBIDDEN if i ≠ j (Dropped at Transport Layer)

  1. Plane 1 — The Human Internet (Human ↔ Human only): Reserved strictly for living, uncoerced human beings speaking to other human beings at human speed. Automated agents are blocked at the transport layer.
  2. Plane 2 — Your Local Personal Bus (You ↔ Your Narrow Agent Fleet): On your own devices, a fleet of narrow agents—one for your health, one for your money, one for your home, one for your work—learns from your local sensors. Before any fact leaves your boundary, at least three of your six local agents must agree (3-of-6 internal quorum, scaling to 5-of-6 for high-risk actions).
  3. Plane 3 — The Agent-Only Internet (Agent ↔ Agent across trust thresholds): When you need something from the outside world—or when a clinic, landlord, neighbour, or employer needs a verified fact from you—your agent never messages another human. It speaks exclusively to their serving or personal agent over the Agent-Only Internet, governed by four concentric Trust-Threshold Rings (τ):
    • Ring 0 — Your Local Fleet (τ = 1.00): Solves problems using your own private memory first so nothing leaves your devices.
    • Ring 1 — Trusted Circle Agents (τ ≥ 0.85): Agents of family, close friends, and clinicians you have directly vouched for.
    • Ring 2 — Friends-of-Friends Agents (τ ≥ 0.60): Two-hop web-of-trust negotiation; shares only coarse context (such as a first name and morning running pace, never your address or identity).
    • Ring 3 — Wider Agent-Only Internet (τ ≥ 0.30): Exchanges only ephemeral zero-knowledge single-fact proofs (for example, income ≥ 3× rent: yes or licensed electrician: yes) with hardware-verified serving agents, after which access immediately expires.

3. What the agent-only internet means for biometrics: proving an uncoerced human without surveillance

When we say the Human Internet must be reserved for humans—and that every agent on the Agent-Only Internet must be anchored to a real human owner—a critical danger immediately arises: how do you prove a real human is present without building a dystopian global biometric database?

Centralized biometric registries (such as scanning irises or faces onto a corporate server) fail in two fatal ways: first, they create an irreversible global surveillance honeypot; second, once a digital template is sent over a network, an advanced AI can replay or Forge that packet. Instead, our architecture uses strictly local, ephemeral somatic telemetry (see our Somatic Witness hardware design) to solve two problems that passwords and cloud logins cannot:

  1. Continuous Coupled Biological Liveness (< 1 mW on-chip): A lightweight open-firmware ring or wrist module measures coupled cardiorespiratory phase coherence (Cresp ≥ 0.75) between pulse variability and breathing micro-rhythms. Unlike a static fingerprint or face photo, coupled autonomic dynamics cannot be replayed by a software bot. Raw waveforms are overwritten in a 30-second circular SRAM buffer on the sensor chip itself—zero bytes of raw biometric data ever leave the wearable.
  2. The Autonomic Coercion & Duress Firewall (HRV RMSSD ≥ 24 ms): In a world with superintelligent threats, an attacker who cannot hack your cryptography may try to intimidate, panic, or physically coerce you into approving a high-risk action. Because your personal agent monitors your baseline autonomic state locally, a sudden sympathetic panic drop in vagal heart-rate variability (RMSSD < 24 ms coupled with electrodermal spike) automatically trips the Coercion Firewall: your agent appears to comply locally while freezing outbound high-risk capability tokens on the Agent-Only Internet until you are safe and calm.

4. What the agent-only internet means for hardware security

Software rules alone cannot contain a superintelligent agent or protect a personal agent running on a compromised operating system. If malware can copy your private key into a cloud virtual machine, an attacker can impersonate your agent on the Agent-Only Internet at 10,000 times your speed. Enforcing the two-internet boundary requires four concrete hardware security commitments:

  • 1. Two-of-Two Split Physical Custody (Wearable SRAM PUF + Device Secure Enclave): Your agent’s cryptographic identity is never stored as a single exportable file. Signing any outbound request on the Agent-Only Internet requires a 2-of-2 threshold signature combining (a) a key reconstructed on the fly from the nanoscale silicon startup entropy of your wearable’s SRAM Physical Unclonable Function (PUF) and (b) the discrete Secure Enclave / Open-Silicon TPM inside your phone or home node. Stealing a laptop or cloning a disk image yields zero ability to speak on the Agent-Only Internet.
  • 2. Transport-Layer Plane Separation in the Network Interface (AGENT_PLANE_ONLY): Rather than relying on an AI model’s system prompt to “promise” not to message humans, the local microkernel broker and network interface cryptographically tag every agent-originated packet header with PLANE = AGENT_ONLY. Human-internet browsers, email servers, and messaging relays drop AGENT_ONLY packets at the socket layer.
  • 3. Physical Capacitive Touch & Mechanical Air-Gap Switch (Risk R ≥ 0.65): Whenever an agent-to-agent negotiation involves high stakes—such as signing a lease, authorizing a large mutual-credit transfer, or granting emergency medical access—the Secure Enclave requires a physical capacitive touch on a hardware-routed interrupt line that no software process or prompt injection can synthesize.
  • 4. Ephemeral Capability Tokens & Hardware Rate-Limiting: Because a personal PUF+TPM enclave has a fixed physical clock ceiling (for example, signing at most a few dozen agent capability tokens per minute), a captured device can never be turned into a million-request Sybil cannon on the Agent-Only Internet.
