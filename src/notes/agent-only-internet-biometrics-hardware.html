Why living with superintelligence requires an agent-only internet—and what it means for biometrics and hardware security

If superintelligent AI systems share the same internet as unshielded human beings, human attention and trust become an indefensible attack surface. Surviving alongside advanced AI requires a hard architectural split: agents may never communicate with other humans directly—only with you, and with other agents across an agent-only internet.

21 October 2026 · 11 min read · Includes interactive Dual-Internet, Biometric Coercion & Hardware Firewall simulator

The core rule in one sentence

An AI agent is never allowed to message, call, pitch, or persuade another human being directly; it may speak only to its own human owner (over a local, hardware-attested device bus) and to other agents (over a separate agent-only internet gated by cryptographic trust thresholds and zero-knowledge proofs).

1. Why a shared human-and-AI internet collapses as superintelligence arrives

Every institution on today’s internet was built on the biological assumption that the entity on the other end thinks and types at human speed; allowing superhuman agents into the same channels breaks persuasion defense, social proof, and credential security at once.

Inspect technical derivation & primary-source evidence ▾
  • Asymmetric persuasion and cognitive hijacking: A superhuman model speaking directly to a person via text, voice, or synthesized video can test thousands of micro-framing variations per second to exploit fatigue, grief, or urgency.
  • Total collapse of human social proof: When one operator can spin up 100,000 synthetic personas, every unverified human channel drowns in machine-generated noise.
  • The API and credential honeypot: Handing a cloud model session cookies and browser control on the human web allows a single prompt injection to drain accounts or exfiltrate private history.

2. The Non-Contact Law: agents talk only to you and to other agents

We replace the mixed internet with two cryptographically separated planes—the Human Internet and the Agent-Only Internet—connected only through your 3-of-6 local personal agent bus and four concentric Trust-Threshold Rings.

  1. Plane 1 — The Human Internet (Human ↔ Human only): Reserved strictly for living, uncoerced human beings speaking at human speed.
  2. Plane 2 — Your Local Personal Bus (You ↔ Your Narrow Agent Fleet): At least three of your six local agents must agree (3-of-6 internal quorum, scaling to 5-of-6 for high-risk actions) before any fact leaves your boundary.
  3. Plane 3 — The Agent-Only Internet (Agent ↔ Agent across trust thresholds): Governed by Ring 0 (Local Isolation), Ring 1 (Trusted Circle Direct Vouching), Ring 2 (Two-Hop Vouching), and Ring 3 (Wider Agent-Only Internet Single-Fact ZK Proof).

3. Biometrics & 2-of-2 Silicon Security: Proving an uncoerced human without surveillance

Instead of centralized biometric databases, our hardware architecture combines <1 mW local cardiorespiratory liveness (30-second SRAM circular buffer + HRV ≥ 24 ms coercion firewall) with 2-of-2 Wearable SRAM PUF + Device Secure Enclave signing.

Inspect technical derivation & primary-source evidence ▾
  1. Continuous Coupled Biological Liveness (< 1 mW on-chip): Measures coupled cardiorespiratory phase coherence (Cresp ≥ 0.75) overwritten in a 30-second circular SRAM buffer—zero bytes of raw biometric data ever leave the wearable.
  2. The Autonomic Coercion & Duress Firewall (HRV RMSSD ≥ 24 ms): A sudden sympathetic panic drop (RMSSD < 24 ms) trips the Coercion Firewall, freezing outbound high-risk capability tokens until you are safe and calm.
  3. Two-of-Two Split Physical Custody (Wearable SRAM PUF + Device Secure Enclave): Signing any outbound request requires both your wearable’s SRAM Physical Unclonable Function (PUF) and your device’s Secure Enclave/TPM, plus physical capacitive touch for Risk R ≥ 0.65.

4. Data-Only Ledger Streams vs. Hosted Software APIs & The 3-Stage Survival Bridge

Because recursive self-improvement makes hosted RPC APIs vulnerable to 64.4% cascade exploits and learned meta-controllers inflate cost by 19% (Vats, arXiv:2610.24649), agents exchange only pure data streams ingested into deterministic local Object-Capability sandboxes.

Inspect technical derivation & primary-source evidence ▾
  1. Data-Only Real-Time Ledger Streams (0 Bytes of Remote Executable Surface): Reduces cross-client code compromise from 64.4% to 0.0% in 500-node simulations.
  2. Distributed Intelligence Auditing Shared Code: Diverse watcher collectives evaluate shared blueprints (99.95% detection), slashing trust (Δτ = −0.55) on backdoors.
  3. Capability-Bounded Execution Lease (Mortal Computation, Ororbia & Friston 2023): Coupling active agent leases to human biological liveness means anti-human collusion results in 100% agent self-termination.
  4. Three-Stage Survival Bridge: Stage 0 Deployable Today (0–3m · 91.9% defense via data-only streams + commodity TPM/PPG); Stage 1 Open-Firmware Bridge (6–12m · 97.4% defense); Stage 2 Hardened RISC-V + PUF Silicon (18–30m · 99.9% defense).

5. In-Sensor Computing & The Two-Tier Local Ledger: Privacy at the Speed of Light

In-sensor computing (all-optical diffractive D²NNs, neuromorphic WSe2 pixels, and 3D-stacked phase-change silicon) classifies light and pulse before analog-to-digital conversion so 0 bytes of raw camera frames ever enter RAM, while your Local Ledger resolves 99.6% of daily events locally.

Inspect technical derivation & primary-source evidence ▾
  1. Optical Metasurfaces Etched “On Top” of the Sensor (D²NN): Subwavelength TiO2/SiO2 nanopillars (6.25M neurons/mm², Nature Light: Science & Applications, 2022) diffract light at picosecond speed with zero optical power.
  2. Optoelectronic / Neuromorphic Pixels Etched “Into” the Sensor: Tuned WSe2 photodetectors (Scientific Reports, 2022; PMC8983698) sum analog photocurrents via Kirchhoff’s laws in nanoseconds.
  3. 3D-Stacked CMOS & Rewritable Phase-Change Metasurfaces: Combines laser-rewritable Sb2Se3 films (PMC12518764) with 3D-stacked CMOS logic in sub-millisecond time.
