Where the World Stands vs. CIN Co-Intelligence: The Last Resort Before a Hard Pause

Human biology evolves on a millennial cycle (~40 bits/s conscious processing), while frontier AI task horizons double every 4.5 months (METR, 2026). This blueprint visually compares the world’s seven major institutional actors against CIN’s Two-Internet Isolation, Capability-Bounded Execution Lease (Hinton, 2022) (Ororbia & Friston, 2023), and 1-to-1 Pair Intelligence.

2. Lens 1 & Lens 2: SOTA AI Labs & The AI Safety Spectrum (Last Resort Before Pause)

Centralized AI labs optimize for equity valuation and API lock-in, pitting biological humans in direct competition against machine swarms. AI safety and evaluation institutes (METR, Redwood Research, Apollo, UK AISI) provide indispensable empirical diagnostics—yet diagnostics alone cannot prevent deployment or economic displacement. CIN occupies the crucial structural zone immediately before a Global Hard Pause: isolating the Two Internets, binding control 1-to-1 to single humans, and enforcing Capability-Bounded Execution Lease (Hinton, 2022) (Ororbia & Friston, 2023).

3. Lens 3 & Lens 4: Government Institutions & Public Research Universities

Legislatures operate on 24–60 month statutory cycles while relying on payroll taxes that automation erodes. Simultaneously, public universities originate foundational breakthroughs (Transformers, Diffusion, LoRA) yet receive 0% of downstream commercial revenue and face compute starvation when competing against frontier AI for grants.

4. Lens 5 & Lens 6: Private Enterprise & The Unprepared Non-Digital Majority

Private businesses that rent cloud AI wrappers leak their trade secrets and pay 15%–40% platform tolls—training conglomerate models to disintermediate them. Even more urgently, 60%+ of humanity works in physical trades, agriculture, and resource extraction where automated buyer cartels can weaponize asymmetric intelligence to depress local terms of trade.
