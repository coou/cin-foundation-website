Thirty lives on the network: how 30 diverse people and their agents coordinate

Abstract principles only matter if they work for real people living very different lives. We built a 365-day simulation of thirty distinct people and their personal agents—spanning six continents, ten income brackets, different family structures, and five levels of authority—drawing on our pool of 550 starting principles.

20 October 2026 · Interactive 30-persona simulation · Verified in our deterministic Python test suite

Inequality compression (365 days) Gini drops from 0.87 to 0.58

In today’s extractive economy, the 30-person Gini index worsens to 0.89 as automated robot rents concentrate at the top. On the shared 5D demurrage ledger, inequality falls sharply while zero savings below the $25,000 floor are ever touched.

Care, farming & commons work More than 4x higher thriving

Nurses, midwives, smallholder farmers, teachers, and rainforest guardians see their verified vital, physical-effort, and ecological contributions fairly compensated alongside software and capital.

Unilateral power & botnets 100% of swarm abuses blocked

When the 50,000-robot factory owner or strategic commander attempts a unilateral market cornering or cross-border strike, the risk-scaled consent gate blocks it automatically.
