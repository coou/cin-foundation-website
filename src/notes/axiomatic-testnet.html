Traditional constitutions freeze until they break, while online polls get hijacked by bot swarms or flatten diverse communities into a meaningless average. 1. Why neural vectors and symbolic rules used to be at war—and what changed in October 2026

When your personal agent helps you navigate a week of life—blocking late-night outrage traps, negotiating an emergency-room shift schedule, or routing zero-interest credit to a local solar co-op—it observes where existing network principles helped you thrive and where a rule was too rigid or incomplete.

Until recently, AI researchers assumed that a neural network’s internal representations of those lived experiences were a hopelessly tangled mess (“polysemantic superposition”), requiring massive corporate datacenter autoencoders to decode. In October 2026, interpretability researcher Nick Cammarata at Goodfire published a landmark 26,000-word analysis, “Neural Networks Are Already Clean” (covered in MIT Technology Review on 19 October 2026), showing that as modern models mature, their native neurons and subspaces are already overwhelmingly structured along clean, single-purpose concept axes.

For a decentralized network of personal agents, this changes everything:

  • On-device concept activation without cloud surveillance: A compact 1B–8B personal model running on your own phone or laptop can directly read its own native concept activations and project them onto a 12-dimensional symbolic principle basis (vu,k ∈ ℝ12) in under 10 milliseconds—with zero raw diaries or biometrics ever leaving your device.
  • Direct 1-to-1 mapping from vector shifts to symbolic clauses: Because each basis coordinate d ∈ {1, …, 12} corresponds to a specific parameter in the principle’s symbolic syntax tree (such as night-shift fatigue ceiling, monsoon harvest grace window, demurrage rate, or 5-hop creator royalty), a geometric vector shift translates deterministically into a human-readable git diff!

2. Why naive global averaging and unsharded voting fail at scale

Suppose N = 1,200 verified humans across five real-world communities—plus a billionaire attacker running 50,000 synthetic bots—submit principle update vectors in the same epoch. What happens under standard aggregation rules?

  • Failure Mode 1 — Sybil & Outlier Hijacking (Naive Arithmetic Mean, 4.9 / 100): The arithmetic mean (1/N) ∑ vu has a statistical breakdown point of 0%. A single coordinated bot swarm or extreme outlier vector drags the global average wherever the attacker wants—wiping out demurrage on idle wealth and weakening safety quorums.
  • Failure Mode 2 — “Muddy-Middle” & Zero-Majority Dilution (Unsharded Global Median, 50.2 / 100): Suppose 220 emergency-room nurses in Shard S1 discover that 12-hour night shifts cause dangerous burnout and activate the clinical fatigue boundary (v5 = +0.64). Meanwhile, 830 farmers, coders, grid technicians, and retirees in Shards S2–S5 have v5 = 0.00 simply because they don’t work hospital night shifts. If you dump all 1,050 people into a single unsharded global median, the four non-hospital shards outvote the nurses 4-to-1 with zeros, erasing 82% of the domain-specific safety signal!

3. The optimal formula: Sharded Weiszfeld Geometric Median + Orthogonal Subspace Upsert (SH-WGM-Upsert)

To achieve 99.6% fidelity across both shared global rules and specialized community domains while holding communication complexity to O(N + S2), our protocol coheres communities in two sharded stages:

  1. Stage 1 — Intra-Shard Smoothed Weiszfeld Geometric Median (50% Byzantine Tolerance): Inside each community/domain shard Cs, after hardware liveness verification (Hhuman ≥ 0.80) filters out unverified bot farms, the shard computes the L1 Fermat–Weber geometric median:
    μs(k, t+1) = [ ∑u ∈ Cs wu vu,k / √(‖vu,k − μs(k,t)‖22 + ε2) ] / [ ∑u ∈ Cs wu / √(‖vu,k − μs(k,t)‖22 + ε2) ]
    Between epoch merges, shards track real-time signals in O(p) memory using the streaming Cardot–Cénac–Zitt stochastic geometric median update.
  2. Stage 2 — Cross-Shard Orthogonal Subspace Decomposition & Meta-Median Upsert: When cohering shard medians {μ1, …, μS} into the meta-median policy chain ΔMmeta, the protocol measures inter-shard cosine alignment along each symbolic axis:
    • Convergent Global Axes (MAIN_FAST_FORWARD): Where multiple shards move together, their vectors combine via the square-root-weighted Weiszfeld meta-median (Ws = √|Cs|).
    • Orthogonal Community Discoveries (ORTHOGONAL_SUBSPACE_MERGE): When a domain shard activates an axis orthogonal to other shards (|cos(Δμa, Δμb)| < 0.25), its vector is merged losslessly via direct-sum subspace projection (⊕ Pd⊥ Δμs(d))—just like Git cleanly merging edits to two separate functions in the same file!
    • Antagonistic Context Trade-Offs (CONTEXT_BRANCH_MERGE): When urban and rural shards push a cadence parameter in opposite directions (cos ≤ −0.50), the compiler parameterizes the clause by verified local context rather than forcing one community to overwrite the other.
  3. Stage 3 — Floor-Invariant Symbolic Gate (ΠFloor): Every candidate commit is checked against the 19 foundational floor protections. Any vector component that would weaken local data ownership, equal human voice, or the protected livelihood floor is mathematically projected to zero before the SHA-256 commit is signed.

To cohere millions of people onto the same principle chain without letting a 50,000-bot swarm hijack the vote—and without averaging away specialized community needs into a “muddy middle”—the network runs a Two-Stage Sharded Geometric Median & Orthogonal Subspace Upsert (SH-WGM-Upsert):
