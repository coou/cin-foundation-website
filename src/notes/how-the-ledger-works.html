How the shared ledger records value, circulates idle wealth, and trains bounded agents

If a ledger records everything in public, it becomes a panopticon. If it records only a single dollar price, it lets monopolies underpay care work and hoard automated wealth. Here is how a two-layer ledger solves both—and serves as a verified, poison-resistant training ground for personal agents.

20 October 2026 · 10 min read · With an interactive 5D compensation & demurrage calculator

In short

Your raw life history stays on your own device in a Personal Ledger signed by your hardware sensor and personal agent. Only zero-knowledge proofs of what happened cross to the Shared Cooperative Ledger, where five levels of agent permissions govern who can read, verify or clear transactions. Human productivity is recognized across five irreducible dimensions, idle surplus above a protected savings floor gently melts back into the commons unless invested in zero-interest community projects, and four quality gates ensure agents learn only from verified 30-day human thriving.

1. Two ledger layers and five levels of agent permissions

When you finish an eight-hour nursing shift, repair a neighbour’s roof, publish open-source code, or protect a local watershed, how does that event get recorded without exposing your private life?

  1. Layer 1 — Your Personal Ledger (on your device): Your personal sensor module and device chip co-sign the raw event (the hours worked, the physical effort, or whether a purchase actually helped you thirty days later) into an encrypted log that never leaves your hardware.
  2. Layer 2 — The Shared Cooperative Ledger (federated across member co-operatives): When you want your work recognized or a community need matched, your outward agent generates a mathematical proof that the work occurred—co-signed by the person or co-operative that received it—and posts only that proof to the shared ledger.

No agent gets a master key to both layers. Instead, agents operate inside five strict permission rings:

  • Level 0 — Local Observer (air-gapped): Can read your hardware sensor and append to your Personal Ledger on your device. Has zero internet access.
  • Level 1 — Personal Negotiator: Reads only the high-level tastes and needs you approve; posts anonymized requests and settles everyday exchanges on the Shared Ledger.
  • Level 2 — Independent Watcher: Operated by diverse makers; cross-checks supply-chain, environmental and effort proofs without seeing private identities or holding money.
  • Level 3 — Cooperative Clearing Agent: Runs a member-owned co-operative’s clearing rules, protected savings floors and demurrage recycling under a temporary, auditable mandate.
  • Level 4 — High-Risk Multi-Agent Coordinator: Locked by default; can only execute when unlocked by the risk-scaled consent gate of the people in the blast radius.

2. Recording human productivity across five dimensions

In today’s economy, a financial speculator or an ad-tech monopoly can capture millions while an emergency-room nurse, a rural teacher, a family farmer or an open-source maintainer struggles to pay rent. That happens because a single price measures bargaining power and artificial scarcity, not human contribution.

On the shared ledger, every verified contribution carries a five-dimensional value receipt:

  1. Vital thriving: Direct provision of healthcare, nursing, child and elder care, nourishing food, housing, and clean energy—confirmed by the people who received it.
  2. Verified human effort & craft: Physical and cognitive exertion attested by the worker’s own edge sensor module, ensuring hard human work always commands dignity.
  3. Knowledge & forecasting: Teaching, open science, software, art, and proven forecasting accuracy.
  4. Ecological stewardship: Measured reduction of waste, pollution, and watershed or forest decay, confirmed by independent watchers.
  5. Foundation lineage credit: Automatic upstream royalties whenever a downstream company, robot fleet or AI model builds on your writing, code, art or craft.

3. How demurrage works on the ledger—without hurting ordinary savers

If money can be hoarded forever at compound interest, wealth inevitably concentrates into a few hands—especially when a few owners control fleets of automated robots. Nearly a century ago, the Austrian town of Wörgl showed another way: currency that carries a small holding fee if left sitting idle, so it circulates rapidly into real work and community projects.

Early versions of demurrage had a flaw: a flat holding fee hurt retired people and families saving for an emergency. Our ledger design fixes that with three rules:

  • A protected personal security floor ($25,000 purchasing-power equivalent): Every person’s baseline savings buffer has 0% demurrage. Retirees and working families never watch their rainy-day savings melt.
  • Continuous demurrage on idle surplus & robotic capital above the floor: Hoarded balances above the security floor gently decay (for example, 8% per year), and large industrial robot fleets contribute a direct automation share—flowing automatically into a universal compute and livelihood dividend for every member.
  • Zero-demurrage exemption for productive community investment: Whenever anyone lends their surplus at zero interest to a community housing co-op, solar microgrid, or local enterprise, the invested amount is exempt from demurrage while it is helping build the commons.

4. How the ledger trains bounded agents without data poisoning

Because the shared ledger holds verified records of what people needed, what actions were taken, and what genuinely helped them, it is the ideal training ground for personal and cooperative agents. But only if bad data and manipulation are kept out. Every record must pass four hard verification gates before an agent can learn from it:

  1. Gate 1 — Hardware liveness & multi-party witness: Any record lacking a valid sensor liveness proof and independent counterparty confirmation is rejected at the door. Bot farms get zero training weight.
  2. Gate 2 — 30-day human fulfillment label: Agents never train on instant clicks or short-term prices; they train on whether the person reported genuine satisfaction and lower stress thirty days later.
  3. Gate 3 — Privacy stripping & compute bounds: Differential privacy strips out anything that could identify an individual before shared patterns are learned, and strict compute caps prevent any model from growing beyond its mandate.
  4. Gate 4 — Independent watcher check: Every training update is checked against independent measures of human freedom and wellbeing. If a model gets better at a score while human autonomy drops, the update is rolled back automatically.
