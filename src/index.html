Superintelligence is coming.Everyone needs an agent of their own.

Advanced AI is arriving faster than our institutions can adapt. The Collective Intelligence Network is building the layer between people and AI: frontier models kept contained, a personal agent loyal to each person, and a collective intelligence that coordinates better than the systems we have now.

Why now

The old safeguards assume humans.

Our laws, markets, identity checks and elections were built for people who act at human speed and pay a cost for every action. Capable AI agents break those assumptions, and they already use the same internet, phones and services we do.

01

AI that improves itself

We think AI systems that can improve themselves may arrive within a few years, possibly as soon as 2027. We may be wrong about the date. We would rather be early than unprepared.

02

Central systems are targets

Governments, banks and platforms concentrate power in a few places. To a capable adversarial AI, every point of central control is an attack surface.

03

People are left defenceless

No one can personally check every message, offer, request and claim an AI can produce. People need something on their side that works at the same speed.

Our goals

Six goals, one direction.

A future where advanced AI serves people: each person protected, communities coordinating freely, and the costs of what we do visible to everyone.

  1. 01

    Make AI safe through containment and fractured alignment

    Instead of one all-knowing model with free rein over the internet, we break intelligence into bounded parts. Frontier models run contained. Each person has a small personal agent trained for them alone, which protects them from adversarial agents and is held accountable when its person is harmed.

    • Containment: air gaps, one-way data channels, no shared hardware, isolated simulations, observable training
    • A personal agent for every person, trained on that one person, answering only to them
    • Separate channels for agents and for people, so the human internet stays human
  2. 02

    Replace top-down bureaucracy with collective intelligence

    Most people feel their governments and corporations no longer serve them. Agents can make direct participation practical: they simulate proposals, tell their person when an outcome works against their needs, and shield people from manipulation through fear, identity or dogma. They never judge what anyone believes.

    • Agents participate on equal terms and stand between people and any system that could abuse them
    • Transparent institutions are rewarded
    • Over generations, people choose cooperatives and decentralized organizations over top-down ones. Better systems win by being chosen, not by force.
  3. 03

    Replace opaque money with honest, multi-dimensional value

    Today's money is one number moving one way. It hides the human and environmental cost of what we buy. We are designing a shared ledger where consumers and producers leave feedback on many dimensions, and where the origin of goods, data and AI work can be traced.

    • Provenance for products, data and AI execution, checked by many independent agents
    • Agents that match people to work they are suited for, or offer training where people are needed
    • Shared resources held by many member-owned cooperatives, never one central treasury
  4. 04

    Train aligned agents in large simulations

    Before agents act in the world, they should prove themselves in sandboxes: many individual agents coordinating, testing their limits, and learning under verifiable rewards for transparency, communication, coordination and human survival.

    • Sandboxed worlds where failure costs nothing real
    • Penalties that make an agent's survival depend on the wellbeing of people
    • Rewards that can be checked, not just claimed
  5. 05

    Forecast where AI and society are heading

    We track signs of danger and signs of progress in AI and in how societies govern themselves. We publish our forecasts and keep score, so it is clear where research is most needed and which problems are urgent.

    • Warning signs and good news get equal attention
    • Forecasts stated in advance, scored as they resolve, misses included
  6. 06

    An interface that brings you what matters

    In a world flooded with AI-generated content, no one should have to filter everything alone. Your agent brings you a simple window of quests and critical information, tuned to your own feedback. It starts on screens today and could move to brain-computer interfaces later, always with your consent.

    • Your agent chooses what to surface for you, and you can see why
    • You can switch it off at any time
Fractured alignment

Many small minds, each answerable to someone.

A single model that knows everything and touches everything is a single point of failure. We think safety comes from splitting intelligence into parts, each with its own boundary, its own priors and someone it answers to.

When a business wants agents to use its service, it doesn't open its doors to every bot. Its serving agent answers the questions of client agents. Your personal agent holds your credentials, proves only the one fact a service needs, and decides what to reveal. Frontier models reach the human world only through these layers. Some work happens in contained environments that a lab opens to chosen people and their agents for a specific purpose.

The answer to more powerful AI is not more authority for AI. It is narrower privileges, independent checks and agents bound to people.

Diverse, not uniformAgents built on different models check one another, so one flaw cannot spread everywhere at once.
Small mistakes stay smallAn agent's authority is limited by how many people its actions can affect, so errors are caught while they are still small.
A signature isn't the truthA cryptographic signature proves which computation ran, not that its result is true. Claims are checked by independent observers.

Signals we watch

Toward danger, or toward alignment?

We are building an observatory of where AI and society are heading. Warning signs get at least as much space as good news. Nothing is published yet, so there are no numbers here.

Signs of danger

!Advanced AI concentrated in a few providers
!Agents acting freely on the human internet with no one answerable
!Self-improving systems without independent oversight
!Network shutdowns and the erosion of democratic participation

Signs of progress

+Interpretability research and honest disclosure by AI labs
+Capable models that people run on their own devices
+Containment and isolation practices adopted for frontier systems
+Institutions that openly show how they serve their people
Lines we hold

Whatever we build, these don't move.

Your data is yours.It lives on your devices. You can see it, take it with you and delete it.
Your agent works only for you.Services get proof of the one fact they need, never your data.
No one is ranked.People are never scored into a ladder of worth.
Beliefs are protected.We shield people from manipulation; we never judge or try to change what they believe.
Anyone can leave.People and communities can walk away or start their own version at any time.
No force, no attention traps.We never use physical force, and never measure or maximize your time or engagement.
