                Check an assistant that sees your screen

                Test whether an assistant that reads your screen may click or type somewhere. It blocks password and card fields, and on-screen text that tries to give the assistant instructions. This is a preview in your browser: nothing is clicked or typed, and the check only matches words.

                Test what an assistant may do on a site

                Check whether an assistant's proposed action stays inside its task boundary (allowed domains, scopes, and verbs), blocks requests that declare credentials, and flags sensitive actions for your confirmation. This is a preview in your browser: nothing is run, and the check trusts what the request says about itself.

                Allowed for this test: domains github.com, api.github.com, docs.github.com; actions read, search, open a page, change something, sign in to another app (asks you first), set up automatic notifications (asks you first); scopes repo:read, issues:read, docs:read.
